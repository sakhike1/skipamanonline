Skipaman Radio music goes here.

Only add tracks you have the rights to use: royalty-free music, or music from an artist
who has agreed in writing to feature on the store. Do not add commercial songs without a licence.

Then list each file in the VIBES list at the top of src/components/ui/SkipamanRadio.tsx, e.g.
  { id: 'amapiano', label: 'Amapiano', tracks: [{ title: 'Song', artist: 'Artist', src: '/audio/song.mp3' }] }

Until a vibe has at least one track, it is hidden. While no vibe has tracks, the radio button does not appear.
